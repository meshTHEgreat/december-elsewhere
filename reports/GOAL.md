# December, elsewhere | execution goal
Deliver one portable, offline decision tool for three adults departing MED on 5 December 2026, covering Baku, Tbilisi, Budapest, Vienna and Prague, returning before 13 December. Budget target SAR 4,000–6,000 per person. Scope: research, visuals, stays, maps, realistic itineraries, local transport, cost comparison, audit and two independent fresh verification rounds. Egypt excluded.

## Acceptance
- All five cities and baseline lodging options remain discoverable with sourced, historical prices.
- Flight schedule possibilities, whole-trip totals and booking uncertainty are clearly differentiated.
- Routes start at public landmarks or stations when exact apartment pin is unavailable.
- Photographs must be real, sourced and licensed for local distribution. Exact property photos may be left as external links when rights are not established; record that limitation.
- All core features work from file:// with no internet: city navigation, stays, map, itinerary, calculator, comparison and evidence. Respond to keyboard, mobile and reduced motion.
- Independent review, material findings resolved, and two fresh end-to-end rounds with evidence. Record any remaining limitations.
- Optional public deployment requires an authorized repository and approved public content.

## Gate state

Keep the goal open until two consecutive independent fresh-browser runs complete the end-to-end checklist against the same release. Each round must use a clean browser profile and open the standalone artifact with networking disabled. Record browser, build SHA, assertions and failures. Any material failure resets the count to zero. A preview browser check or isolated-script check contributes evidence but does not increment the count. Track executed runs and blockers in `VERIFICATION.md`.
