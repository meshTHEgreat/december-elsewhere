# 02. Navigation, cover and atlas

Measured commit `6f017c5` from the local Pages build at `http://127.0.0.1:18768/`, in Chrome 157 using fresh contexts. All six sizes use English LTR. The first three simulate touch. The script uses `getBoundingClientRect` for geometry and one Range per rendered character for text wrapping. It preserves raw results, including foreground and nearest solid background contrast. Photographic text has variable contrast and these solid-color ratios do not cover it.

## First view

Visitors need to choose a city, see its price, and jump to a part of that city. The map is useful for visitors who want to compare locations. Put a map jump beside the city heading on phones. Keep the complete city list as the primary path. Keep the 3D globe optional.

## Interaction and states

- The seven compact nav links are 44 px high. The open Baku menu is 224 × 324.4 px in portrait and 320 × 192.4 px in landscape. All seven link centers receive pointer hits. Keyboard Tab from the summary reaches Arrival. Escape closes the disclosure and restores focus. The compact menu has no icons beyond its chevron and no badges. Keep the nav sequence from the journey. [320 menu](shots/320-chapter-menu.jpg) · [667 opened menu](shots/667-chapter-menu.jpg)
- On desktop, the seven links share the same top and bottom edge. Label names match one section title exactly out of seven. “Itinerary” opens “Day by day”, and “Photos” opens “The photo journal”. “Arrival”, “Stays”, “Walks”, and “Winter” open expanded editorial labels. Use the glossary to choose matching functional labels where useful. The shortest action labels are easy to scan. [Wide navigation](shots/1440-chapter-chrome.jpg)
- At all six widths, the first keyboard Tab focuses the 125 × 46 px skip link. No measured page or chapter state has document-level horizontal scroll. Solid-color text of measured chrome targets stays above 6.12:1. Footer links and the atlas actions all reach 44 × 44 px. [390 footer](shots/390-footer.jpg) · [1440 footer](shots/1440-footer.jpg)
- In all 30 static-atlas selections, the route, endpoint and city action update. In all 30 globe selections the active label follows the selector. The globe loads on demand and is removed by “View map”. Pressing the chapter action selects the city and focuses its main heading. Under simulated WebGL failure, the flat atlas remains visible and the status renders at all widths. Reduced-motion mode preserves all controls. [390 globe](shots/390-globe.jpg) · [667 fallback](shots/667-atlas-fallback.jpg)
- The cover title wraps on its intentional break at all sizes. The 320 px trip facts use 31 / 15 rendered characters. They keep the dates together. The atlas lead needs the smallest layout adjustment. [320 cover](shots/320-cover.jpg) · [390 cover](shots/390-cover.jpg)
