# 05 | Independent mobile design review

I reviewed the actual baseline images for the cover, navigation, city list, atlas, Stays, Walks, Winter, photo dialog, Budget, Compare and Sources, then checked the relevant source before opening the nine draft proposals. The baseline captures use Chrome 157 on `6f017c5`. I concentrated on 320 × 725, 390 × 844 and 667 × 375, with 768 and desktop evidence for secondary effects. Numerical measurements come from the linked baseline audits unless marked as a source calculation. The review used existing browser captures and source. I made no app changes.

Keep UI-01 through UI-08 in the release with the revisions below. Keep UI-09 deferred. The three strongest mobile priorities are a budget field that stays visible while editing, a route you can see immediately after selecting it in landscape, and a winter preview with visible controls and legible text in landscape. The full-bleed Prague cover, paper and forest palette, five compact city rows, and the inline travel tools already establish the identity. Leave those compositions in place. ## Proposal decisions

### UI-01 | Approve with a full focus and currency test

The 191.67 px sticky summary at 320 covers 25 px of a 44 px baggage input. The 46.86 px rental disclosure can disappear entirely at 320 and 390. Keep the pin and put the per-adult and three-person amounts on two compact, labeled rails. Keep the contingency explanation adjacent to the figures or the inputs. The supported maximum from the source is seven per-adult fields and seven group fields at SAR 150,000 each, yielding **SAR 4,200,000 for three and SAR 1,400,000 per adult**. Verify those amounts as well as all five default totals at 320, 390 and the 768 split. Check forward and reverse Tab, the rental summary, reset, focus ring clearance and the visual viewport with a numeric keyboard where available. Base focus clearance on the actual height of both sticky bars and the total. Preserve live totals and the current thresholds. ### UI-06 | Revise the focus and landscape composition before approval

I disagree with giving Close focus if named scene controls appear later in the compact panel and the motion is still playing. Focus a labeled, visible preview region so Tab follows the controls in reading order, or put Close first in the panel and keep later controls in sequence. The existing stage starts with 271 of 365 px visible at 667 and zero of the 44 px focused trigger. Roughly 271 px is available below the two bars. A 211 px scene and 44 px Close can fit with about 16 px left, while a third 44 px tier needs its own reachable row. The source sets a 654 px compact threshold and a wide artboard of 736 × 338 px with 15 px body copy. Merely fitting the wide artboard to 460 px renders that detail at about **9.4 px**. Use a shallow landscape composition with legible rendered text near the current compact detail size instead. Keep the 320 and 390 composition full. Test both sequences, including the longest note, scene changes and the final frame. Make direct scene selection stop any pending automatic advance that would overwrite it. Reduced-motion readers can select every still scene. Test Escape and Close for a visible return to the trigger, loading and offline fallback, and opening the other preview while the first request is pending. ### UI-07 | Revise what goes into the disclosure

The first budget action starts below the 390 viewport in all five cities, at y990–1250. Baku puts a 304 px outdoor recommendation above the priced options. Lead with the two real properties and place the additional outdoor option beneath. I disagree with putting any condition that changes a booking choice solely inside “Listing notes.” Keep the date and scope of observed pricing, beds and adult occupancy, and the exact outdoor status with the two main actions. “Listed · confirm access” is more useful near the booking link than a bare “Listed.” Use the disclosure for supplementary policy or logistical details. Preserve the dated caveat that these are observed prices, direct links to genuine room galleries, unpriced alternatives and their caveats, and the transfer from the stay to Budget and the public walk anchor. Exercise transfer after expanding notes. On wider views align both price rows with the actual titles intact. ### UI-08 | Approve with precise statuses and honest range rendering

Keep the semantic side-by-side table and expanded flights/entry/weather state as selectors change. At 320, local transport labels take three lines and the long outdoor descriptions take up to five. Abbreviate repeated unit words while retaining distinctions such as private, shared, nearby, listed with access to confirm and unverified. The measured band has 1.37:1 contrast against its track. Use a distinguishable edge or fill and keep the visible SAR 4,000–6,000 key. The source’s linear range can display the 2,000 SAR band at less than half a pixel on the narrow screen at the maximum legal budget. At that extreme, preserve honest scale and provide a readable textual range and per-city status. Removing the card fill can give the first table rows more space if spacing is reduced as well. The evidence panel’s 17 of 18 Baku links and 42 of 48 photography links miss 44 px in at least one dimension. Keep all 92 city references and 24 photo entries, dates, license links, attribution and method assumptions. Give repeated source and license actions an accessible name with the photo context, and keep them discoverable in individually opened disclosures. Split the 201-character wide methodology lines by topic on the existing reading rail. ### UI-09 | Defer the ultrawide rail and track phone map lettering separately

The equal 664 px outer bands at 2560 surround a centered 1232 px rail, and the cover photograph and dark chapter closing band already reach the viewport edges. A wider rail has low value for the phone-first release. The 9.3–9.6 px phone atlas labels are a separate problem. Deferral is acceptable while the city selector stays fully legible and the chosen destination remains recognizable on the map. A small-map fix can start by adjusting SVG label offsets and checking collisions before raising lettering toward 11 px. Keep the pins and bearings geographically exact.
