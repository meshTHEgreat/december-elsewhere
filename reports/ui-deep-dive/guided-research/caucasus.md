## Baku, 5–12 December

### Keep Gobustan and Absheron as a day trip from Baku

The owner's [shared link](https://gyg.me/E7aodHKh) returned HTTP 301 to GetYourGuide activity 517765. The canonical activity page was readable in the research browser. It describes a 7.5–9 hour Baku round trip from Baku Heritage Tours/Travel. Stops include Bibiheybat Mosque, Gobustan rock art and mud volcanoes, Ateshgah Fire Temple and Yanar Dag. The latter is Absheron's natural fire site. It is a separate outing from the Greater Caucasus mountains. The Heydar Aliyev Center is an exterior sightseeing and photo stop on the listing. [Exact tour listing](https://www.getyourguide.com/baku-l1408/baku-gobustan-mud-volcano-fire-mount-temple-lunch-entry-t517765/) · [Azerbaijan Tourism Board on Ateshgah and Yanar Dag](https://azerbaijan.travel/sites-of-ancient-fire-leave-a-burning-legacy)

The listing advertises private or small groups, live guide languages including English and Arabic, and several pickup choices. It names 191 Lev Tolstoy for the standard start. Select the right package before assuming hotel pickup, a guide on the private option, lunch or site admissions. The inclusion list shows off-road access fees for the mud-volcano transfer, but the route also displays an extra-fee label at the mud volcanoes. Confirm any remaining fee and the wet-weather substitute with the provider. Mud volcanoes can be skipped after rain. [Tour inclusions and conditions](https://www.getyourguide.com/baku-l1408/baku-gobustan-mud-volcano-fire-mount-temple-lunch-entry-t517765/) · [Azerbaijan Tourism Board on the mud volcanoes](https://azerbaijan.travel/explore-mud-volcanoes-near-baku)

### Give the northern mountains one or two nights

Shahdag Mountain Resort is 212 km from Baku. Azerbaijan's official tourism guide places it about 29 km from Qusar, also written Gusar. The resort lists a Baku International Bus Terminal to Qusar bus and a taxi connection onward, plus the named partner Go2shahdag. Verify current timetables and three-adult baggage capacity. [Shahdag transport](https://shahdag.az/en/how-to-get-here) · [Azerbaijan Tourism Board ski guide](https://azerbaijan.travel/storage/pages/2936/608a4d97-c09e-4e14-9a44-f811bff27b0d.pdf)

If you want a one-day comparison, AZ Legacy Travel advertises a 10–11 hour northern excursion from Baku. Laza and Shahdag are tied to the chosen option, and Shahdag has roughly two hours of free time when selected. That is useful for sampling the area. Choose an overnight if you want a flexible morning and time for a second local stop. [GetYourGuide northern day trip](https://www.getyourguide.com/baku-l1408/baku-guba-sahdag-tour-lunch-included-and-laza-waterfall-t899705/)

One-night plan: drive out **9 December**, stay in the resort or Qusar, and return **10 December**. A second night gives a weather buffer if you return **11 December** and sleep in Baku ahead of the **12 December** flight. The resort publishes a Shahdag to Laza and Suvar waterfall vehicle itinerary. Ask whether it operates in December, who leads it, what the fee includes, and where the run starts. A guide can choose a suitable short village route if conditions allow. For a cultural option based in Quba, ask for a guided visit to the Museum of Mountain Jews in the Red Settlement and confirm opening hours before setting out. Resort winter skiing and its winter activity entries were marked “Opening soon” during the check. Check snow, lifts and roads before paying. Select lodging for three adults and confirm any private terrace or balcony with the property. ## Tbilisi, 5–11 December

### Use a guided day if you want the main sights

TRIPSTER GE lists a 10.5-hour return journey from Tbilisi. It meets at Gorgasali Square and covers the Zhinvali Reservoir, Ananuri, Gudauri and Stepantsminda. English and Russian guides are listed. The final ascent to Gergeti Trinity Church uses an optional paid 4×4, and the listing describes lunch as optional. Confirm current terms, itinerary and availability. [GetYourGuide Kazbegi day trip](https://www.getyourguide.com/tbilisi-l1379/from-tbilisi-to-kazbegi-ananuri-gudauri-gergeti-t573712/)

### Stay in Stepantsminda for a slower mountain visit

Rauls Company lists a guided outbound trip with luggage space, a drop-off in Stepantsminda and a return on a later day. The public listing asks travelers to confirm their return at least a day before the pickup. Check the agreed round-trip fare and both pickup points. Arrange your own overnight stay and confirm how far ahead your return needs to be reserved. [GetYourGuide flexible-return trip](https://www.getyourguide.com/stepantsminda-l2762/from-tbilisi-kazbegi-gudauri-tour-with-flexible-return-t1431482/)

Travel up on **9 December**, stay one night and return to Tbilisi on **10 December**. Two nights would mean leaving **8 December** and replacing the Kakheti day. Sleep in Tbilisi on 10 December for a buffer ahead of the 11 December departure. From Stepantsminda, ask the Kazbegi National Park visitor center about road and trail access and how to find a certified local guide. Choose Gergeti if access is clear, or a village walk and the local history museum if the guide advises staying lower. A local 4×4 driver and an interpretive guide are different services, so ask which is included. Georgia Travel recommends certified local help for winter mountain activities. Snow and wind can restrict the Gudauri–Kobi road. The Mountain Trails Agency announces Gudauri's official ski-season opening on **20 December 2026**, after these trip dates. Rooms Kazbegi shows a shared outdoor terrace. Confirm occupancy, beds and winter access before booking.
