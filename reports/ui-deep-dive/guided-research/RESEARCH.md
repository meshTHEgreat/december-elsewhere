# Local guides and overnight choices

The owner requested the GetYourGuide comparison while the mobile UI was in independent review. The guide research and build used a separate worktree to keep the candidate under review stable.

Each chapter now has a short planning disclosure within Itinerary, with a reason to choose a day trip or a night away. An overnight displaces some city time. The section explains where to find a guide based near the excursion and lets visitors open the source or update Budget. It keeps the original itinerary and price inputs because no date-specific tour or room reservation was verified.

| Chapter | Suggested decision | Bookable or local source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baku | Keep Gobustan and the Absheron fire sites as a day trip. Consider one or two nights near Shahdag for daylight and an outing from the resort or Qusar if roads permit. | The supplied short link resolves to GetYourGuide product 517765. The listing's times, inclusions and pickup vary by option. [Exact listing](https://www.getyourguide.com/baku-l1408/baku-gobustan-mud-volcano-fire-mount-temple-lunch-entry-t517765/) · [Shahdag Laza outing](https://shahdag.az/en/activity/cip-turlari) |
| Tbilisi | A night in Stepantsminda is worth considering if the road is open. It avoids making both directions part of the same 10½-hour day. | One listing describes a guided outward trip and return transfer on a later day. Arrange lodging separately and confirm the return. [Day tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/tbilisi-l1379/from-tbilisi-to-kazbegi-ananuri-gudauri-gergeti-t573712/) · [Flexible return](https://www.getyourguide.com/stepantsminda-l2762/from-tbilisi-kazbegi-gudauri-tour-with-flexible-return-t1431482/) · [Official winter guidance](https://georgia.travel/winter-hiking-and-snowshoeing-in-kazbegi) |
| Budapest | Keep the H5 day trip to Szentendre. A single night makes sense for an evening on the Danube and a gallery or castle visit the next day. | Use the Budapest private walk on a separate city morning. Ask Szentendre Tourinform for a guide from town and check the Visegrád openings independently. [Private Budapest walk](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/budapest-l29/budapest-private-walking-tour-with-a-local-t101174/) · [Szentendre Tourinform](https://iranyszentendre.hu/en/iranytu/useful-contacts/) · [Visegrád castle](https://iranyvisegrad.hu/en/latnivalo/visegradi-fellegvar-magyarorszag-egyik-legszebb-panoramaja/) |
| Vienna | Bratislava works as a day trip. One night allows time at the evening markets and a local walk the next morning. | The Bratislava Tourist Board links to local guide operators. Vienna's city walk is an independent option on a city day. [ÖBB regional rail](https://www.oebb.at/en/regionale-angebote/ueberregionale-angebote/bratislava-ticket) · [Bratislava guides](https://www.visitbratislava.com/services/regular-tours/) · [Vienna small-group walk](https://www.getyourguide.com/vienna-l7/vienna-small-group-guided-walking-tour-of-city-highlights-t409177/) |
| Prague | Keep the Kutná Hora day trip with a five-night Prague stay. One optional night separates the old town and Sedlec visits. | Compare a coach package with an independently booked train and a guide based at the Italian Court or Sedlec. [Coach tour](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-gb/prague-l10/kutna-hora-from-prague-the-bone-church-st-barbara-s-t76420/) · [Italian Court guides](https://pskh.cz/en/italian-court/) · [Sedlec guides](https://www.sedlec.info/en/tours/day/) |

Sources were checked on 23 November 2026. Dates and times in the chapter are suggestions or published durations as labeled. Tour listings describe the supplier and include a direct link so the traveler can confirm pickup, group size, guide language and location, exact inclusions, cancellation, and the total for three.
